Current research

the thing I think about in the shower

Sequential screening for postpartum depression. At the CMU Lab for AI and Social Impact, in partnership with UPMC, I model over 72,000 pregnancies using EHR and maternal-health app data.

Two threads: XGBoost + SHAP models that predict new-onset depression at each stage of pregnancy (and test whether app data adds signal over EHR alone), and a sequential decision framework that chooses among five graduated interventions using a clinician-informed utility, evaluated against historical care and RL baselines.
