Grace Hopper Celebration India 2024

by Shikha Bhat · 3 min read

GHC India 2024 was a whirlwind! I attended as a delegate from Google: so many inspiring talks, workshops, and incredible women in tech. Here's a rundown of the sessions I managed to catch.

Day 1 · Thursday, November 21

Tech for Social Good: AI industry lightning talks

Indumathy's talk, "The Farmer's Time Machine", really stuck with me. She explained how digital twin technology creates virtual models of farms, so farmers can simulate different scenarios and optimize things like irrigation and crop yields. It was fascinating to see AI applied to such a traditional industry.

Priyanka Sebastian, a researcher from Finland, spoke on "From Imagination to Reality: Tech-Driven Classrooms in a Global Context", about how technology can personalize education and make it more accessible, especially in Finland and India. She highlighted examples like AI tutoring systems and virtual reality learning environments.

Fireside chat: The power of different perspectives

A really engaging conversation about how diversity of thought leads to better problem-solving and innovation in tech.

Keynote: Insight to impact

Vidhya Muralidharan from SAP Labs shared how AI and other technologies are being used to tackle poverty, healthcare access, and environmental sustainability.

Fireside chat: Will AI eat the world?

Revolution or evolution? The speakers explored AI's potential benefits, like efficiency and automation, alongside concerns about job displacement and ethics. They emphasized responsible AI development and upskilling the workforce for a changing landscape.

AI/ML application lightning talks

  • Indrani Bose, LinkedIn: "Content Moderation in LinkedIn's Dynamic Ecosystem", on using AI to filter harmful content while still letting people express themselves, and on balancing free speech with safety and fairness.
  • Anu Ravi, Uber: "Revolutionizing Document Processing at Uber with Gen AI", on automating extraction from invoices and contracts.
  • Message categorization using AI/ML: identifying and filtering fraudulent messages to protect businesses and consumers from scams and phishing.

Day 2 · Friday, November 22

UnConference: The power of presence

A great reminder to slow down and be present in the moment, with a few guided mindfulness exercises.

Opening keynote: Guru Bhat

A very engaging talk on leadership as a driver of innovation and positive impact, and on ethical leadership and building a culture of trust.

Keynote: DURGA, protecting women against violence

Soujanya Madabhushi and Abha Jaiswal gave a moving presentation on using technology to combat violence against women: an app built on three pillars of prevention, immediate action and survivor care, with incident reporting, a support helpline, and training for law enforcement. One of the best sessions I attended.

Classroom: Reimagining software development with generative AI

Mani Chandrasekaran and Chandrasekaran Sivaraman from Amazon talked about Amazon Q Developer and how AI can generate code, automate testing, and even help design user interfaces.

Workshop: Design-think your career

An interactive framework for planning your career path from individual contributor to manager, grounded in your values, strengths, and goals.

Expert talk: Communication as a superpower

Papa CJ was definitely a highlight! He used humor and storytelling to show why communication matters in leadership, with practical tips on building trust and inspiring action.

Fireside chat: Breaking the glass ceiling

Shreya Krishnan (Managing Director, AnitaB.org India) and Bhumi Pednekar had an empowering conversation about challenging norms and breaking barriers for women in tech.

Closing concert

A fantastic way to wrap up! Sushant Divgikar was incredible and the energy was contagious: a perfect celebration of women in tech.
