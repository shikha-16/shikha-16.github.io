Teach Zari with Nirmaan

by Shikha Bhat · 1 min read

In 2019, I joined Nirmaan Organization, an NGO at BITS Goa, as a volunteer in its Teach Zari vertical, which works towards the education of underprivileged children in a nearby village.

The work

Our volunteers taught Math and English to about 20 children from the village. I later became the Project Lead for Teach Zari.

Leading the project

  • Teaching: I taught the students myself.
  • Curriculum: I designed the students' curriculum.
  • Team: I selected and trained a team of 30+ volunteers.

Impact

Three of our students won JNVST government scholarships, which funded their high school education.

This work, along with my efforts for women in STEM, was recognized with the Mantra Award for Social Leadership from the BITS Alumni Association in 2019.
